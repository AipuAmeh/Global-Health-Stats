# Global Health Statistics 
### ** Synthetic Data **

## Research Question
* What is the incidence rate of the top 5 diseases within the past 10 years (2014-2024)?
* Which of the top 5 most common diseases have the highest mortality rate globally? 
* Has there been an increase in mortality over the last 10 years? Which countries had the highest decrease?
* What socioeconomic factors could contribute to the decreasing mortality rate?

## Objective
This milestone focused on leveraging Python to analyze and visualize data to answer specific research questions. The Jupyter Notebook demonstrates the process of:
* Data Retrieval and cleaning
* Data analysis
* Data visualization

## Research Topic
This work seeks to explore what diseases have impacted global countries within the past 10 years and what factors could contribute to these findings.

## Deliverable
A Jupyter Notebook was created to showcase:
* Data retrieval and cleaning 
* Analytical Insights
* Visualizations (grouped bar chart and line chart)

## Key Findings
* The top 5 diseases of this dataset are COVID-19, Cholera, HIV/AIDS, Hepatitis and Parkinson's Disease.
* The most notable change in mortality was a decrease of COVID-19 and HIV/AIDS in 2020.
* Australia, Germany, China, and Indonesia have had the largest decrease in mortality over the past 10 years for COVID-19 and Cholera respectively.
* Healthcare access has made a significant impact on the decreased mortality rate over time.
